dVPN Plan Manager · Solana
Build and operate a subscription dVPN service on Sentinel's Solana program. Register a provider, publish plans, link nodes through your provider daemon, and withdraw earnings — from one console.
Steps to run a plan
  1. 1
    Register provider
    Bond tDVPN and claim your on-chain provider identity.
  2. 2
    Publish a plan
    Price, duration, data cap and connections — one atomic transaction.
  3. 3
    Link nodes
    Your provider daemon keeps the roster and funds the leases automatically.
  4. 4
    Earn
    Subscriptions pay the provider on chain; withdraw revenue any time.

Sign in
Pick how your Solana key is held. Nothing here ever leaves your browser unencrypted.
Sign in with email
Privy issues a Solana embedded wallet for your email. The key lives in Privy's enclave; this server only asks it to sign.
Import a key
Paste a solana-keygen JSON array, a base58 secret key, or a 12/24-word phrase (derived at m/44'/501'/0'/0', the Phantom path). It is encrypted into an httpOnly cookie with AES-256-GCM; the server never stores it.
Create a new wallet
A fresh Solana keypair is generated for this session. You will see the secret key exactly once — back it up before continuing. Fund it with SOL (fees and rent) and tDVPN (bonds and plans).
